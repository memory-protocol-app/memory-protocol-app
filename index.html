How to Play

1. Goal

  1. OBLIVION, a rogue AI, is erasing Philippine history.
  2. Fly your probe through 3 sectors and restore 4 data nodes in each by answering history questions.
  3. Restore all 12 to win. Lose all 4 shields and it’s game over.

2. Controls

  1. Move: W A S D or arrow keys (left stick on touch).
  2. Up / Down: R / F (▲ / ▼ on touch).
  3. Look: drag the mouse or your finger.
  4. Interact: E or Space (INTERACT on touch).
  5. EMP: X.
  6. Pause: Esc or P.

3. Data Nodes

  1. Fly close to a glowing node and press Interact.
  2. Pick the right answer before the timer runs out.
  3. Correct: the node is restored and you score points. Faster answers score more.
  4. Wrong or too slow: you lose 1 shield and the node locks for 4 seconds.
  5. Hint (H): a free clue, once per node.

4. Hunter Drones

  1. Avoid the red drones and their light cones.
  2. If one catches you, type the answer to a history question within 8 seconds.
  3. Correct: the drone is stunned.
  4. Wrong or too slow: you lose 1 shield.
  5. In Sectors 2 and 3, drones chase you.

5. Shields

  1. You have 4.
  2. You lose one for a wrong answer, a failed drone check, or a Neural Pulse hit.

6. Power-Ups

  1. Purple (Decoder): removes 2 wrong answers. Use it inside a question with Z.
  2. Blue (EMP): press X to freeze drones and timers for 8 seconds.
  3. Green (Shield): restores 1 shield.

7. Sectors

  1. Sector 1: 20 seconds per question. Drones never chase.
  2. Sector 2: fog, and chasing drones. The outer red zone gives only 12 seconds and scrambles the text.
  3. Sector 3: 10 seconds per question. A Neural Pulse hits every 15 seconds, so hide in a cyan Pulse Shelter. Nodes float high, so climb.

8. Scoring

  1. Correct node: 100 points plus 10 per second left.
  2. Beating a drone: 50 points.
  3. Pods: 25 points each.
  4. Clearing a sector: 150 points per shield left.

9. Tips

  1. Grab pods early.
  2. Use obstacles to lose chasing drones.
  3. Save EMP for tough questions or close chases.
  4. Play in landscape for maximum visibility.
